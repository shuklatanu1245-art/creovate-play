# 🔮 Creovate Play - Instagram Reels & Real-Time Peer Chat

![Creovate Play Logo](assets/logo.png)

**Creovate Play** ko bilkul clean aur focused bana diya gaya hai:
1. **Real Persons Live Chat:** PeerJS (WebRTC) ke zariye end-to-end direct chatting real logon ke sath.
2. **Instagram Reels Feed:** Instagram ki videos/reels directly embedded hain, plus aap kisi bhi reel ka link paste karke play kar sakte hain!
3. **Floating Mini-Player (Picture-in-Picture):** Instagram Reel background me dekhein jabki aap real person se live chat kar rahe hon.

---

## 👥 Real Persons Se Kaise Baat Karein (How to Chat with Real People)

Creovate Play me direct WebRTC Peer-to-Peer engine integrated hai:

### Step 1: Apna Invite Link ya ID Share Karein
- Top header me aapko aapka unique ID dikhega (e.g. `creo-a78b`).
- **"Invite Friend"** button dabayein — ek clickable direct link copy ho jayega:
  `http://.../index.html?connect=creo-a78b`
- Ye link apne friend ko WhatsApp ya kisi bhi platform par bhej dein.

### Step 2: Instant Real-Time Connection
- Jaise hi aapka friend apne phone ya laptop par link open karega, aap dono instantly **🟢 Live Connected** ho jayenge!
- Ab aap jo bhi message type karenge, wo directly aapke friend ke screen par real time me delivered hoga with WhatsApp blue ticks (`✓✓`)!

### 💡 Instant Multi-Tab Self-Test (Abhi Test Karein):
- Browser me ek naya tab open karein (ya **"Connect" -> "Open 2nd Tab"** dabayein).
- Tab 1 aur Tab 2 aapas me as two real persons instantly chat kar sakte hain!

---

## 🎬 Instagram Videos Kaise Dekhein & Add Karein

1. **Watch Reels:** Feed me trending Instagram Reels directly available hain.
2. **Link Any Instagram Reel:** Top right me **"+ Add Reel"** button par click karein aur Instagram app/browser se kisi bhi Reel ka link paste karein (e.g. `https://www.instagram.com/reel/C5nQ0q0L7f0/`). Reel instantly aapke feed me add ho jayegi!
3. **Float & Chat:** Kisi bhi reel ke neeche **"Float & Chat"** dabayein — reel bottom-right corner me chalu rahegi jabki aap chat karte rahenge.
4. **Send in Chat:** Kisi bhi reel ko ek click me apne friend ke live chat me forward karein!
